# My agent: Personalized Fitness & Weight Management Coach
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps individuals achieve health and weight goals with a catalog of tailored exercise routines, meal plans, and BMI calculations.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: User profile (age, height, weight, gender, calculated BMI history, goal targets, fitness levels, dietary preferences/restrictions)
- Tools: BMI & caloric deficit/surplus calculator, workout routine finder, meal & diet plan generator
- Catalog/UI: Structured workout cards (sets, reps, cardio intervals), meal plan cards with nutritional breakdown, milestone timelines (3-month / 6-month / extended plans) rendered via A2UI
- Image gen: Visual representations of recommended exercises, workout poses, or wholesome meal platters
- Sandbox: Dynamic calculation of BMI, BMR, TDEE, safe weekly weight change trajectories, and timeline forecasting

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): Python sandbox for precise BMR/TDEE and safe caloric timeline calculations, polite and encouraging tone guidelines ensuring empathetic health guidance.
